The Problem & The Vision

Why I Created HostTriangle

Deploying modern frontend applications (React, Next.js, Flutter) has become completely free and instantaneous on services like Vercel and Cloudflare Pages. But backend hosting remains broken: free cloud tiers shut down instances after 15 minutes of inactivity, inflicting brutal 30 to 50-second cold starts on students and indie developers showcasing their work.

Keeping a desktop PC or server running 24/7 consumes 60W–150W of power, requires continuous fan noise, and fails during local power cuts without a heavy UPS.

Then the realization struck: our spare Android phones are already quiet, power-efficient, battery-backed supercomputers. An older phone with an 8-core ARM64 CPU and 6 GB RAM has more raw compute capacity than many standard cloud VPS tiers—drawing less than 2 Watts of electricity.

HostTriangle turns this hardware into an edge compute node. By running an isolated Debian sandbox on Android and exposing your port via an outbound Cloudflare Zero Trust Tunnel, your spare phone serves as a practical, always-on hosting node for your APIs.

Stateless Compute Principle

Keep your database on free managed tiers (Supabase, Neon, MongoDB Atlas), while stateless compute logic runs directly across your devices.

Zero Maintenance Failover

If one node temporarily loses power or reboots, Cloudflare Load Balancing automatically routes traffic to another phone in your cluster.

Under The Hood

Engineered for Production Grade Stability

No hacks, no fragile rooting, and no kernel modifications. Built on standard POSIX virtualization.

01

PRoot v5.1 Userland

Uses ptrace system call interception to provide a rootless chroot environment. The application runs with standard Android UID permissions while believing it has root inside Debian.

02

16 KB Page-Size Native PTY

Compiled with clean-room C code and linked with -Wl,-z,max-page-size=16384. Guarantees 100% compatibility with Android 15 and future Linux kernel architectures.

03

Cloudflare Zero Trust Ingress

The embedded cloudflared daemon establishes encrypted outbound TLS tunnels. Seamlessly punches through carrier CGNAT, home Wi-Fi routers, and mobile firewalls.

PRIVACY & DATA FLOW

Clear, Transparent Data Handling

Your source code and .env files are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not uploaded to a HostTriangle server, because HostTriangle has no server.

Public requests travel through Cloudflare's network, and Cloudflare can see that traffic (TLS terminates at Cloudflare edge before reaching your tunnel). If you connect a cloud database (such as Supabase or Neon), your data lives there.

HostTriangle itself includes no analytics or crash reporting. The bundled cloudflared daemon connects to Cloudflare to run your tunnel.

No HostTriangle Telemetry
HostTriangle includes zero analytics, tracking, or crash reporters. Verified with zero tracking SDKs in the dependency tree.
